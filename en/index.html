02

Modules

Like the parts list on a drawing: each module does one job well, and all of them connect to the same record.

ItemModuleWhat it does
01Work orders and serial numbersPick the product and quantity; the process and drawing revision come from the product. Each part gets its own serial number, and a faulty part is scrapped with one click.
02Station screenOperators record each step from a tablet or computer. Required measurements are prompted on screen, and out-of-limit values show up immediately.
03Process and risk definitionsDefine production steps, control values and failure risks (PFMEA) once. Changes are kept as versions; older work orders stay on their original version.
04Inventory and lot trackingGoods receipt, quality approval, stock counts and shipping. Materials are reserved for work orders from the bill of materials and consumed as production moves on.
05Traceability and certificates of conformityGo from a serial number to the lots it used, and from a lot to every part it went into. The system prepares the certificate of conformity at shipping.
06ReportsFilter work order, quality and material reports and export them to Excel. When an audit comes, you don't waste time hunting for records.
