Production tracking system

Move your shop floor records from paper to screen.

QY Systems brings work orders, every step at every station, inventory and traceability together for small and mid-sized manufacturers. See in seconds which part went through which step, with which material, and by whose hands.

What it does

It replaces shop floor forms, Excel lists and the warehouse logbook with one system. Every record is entered once, and everyone sees the same information.

Work orders and serial numbers

Pick the product and quantity; the process and drawing revision come from the product. Each part gets its own serial number, and a faulty part is scrapped with one click.

Station screen

Operators record each step from a tablet or computer. Required measurements are prompted on screen, and out-of-limit values show up immediately.

Process and risk definitions

Define production steps, control values and failure risks (PFMEA) once. Changes are kept as versions; older work orders stay on their original version.

Inventory and lot tracking

Goods receipt, quality approval, stock counts and shipping. Materials are reserved for work orders from the bill of materials and consumed automatically as production moves on.

Traceability and certificates of conformity

Trace both ways: from a serial number to the material lots used, and from a lot to every part it went into. The system prepares the certificate of conformity at shipping.

Reports

Filter work order, quality and material reports and export them to Excel. When a customer audit comes, you don't waste time hunting for records.

How it works

No big migration project. Start with a single line and run it side by side with your paper records.

  1. We define your process together

    We sit down with you and enter your products, production steps and control values.

  2. Recording starts on the floor

    Operators record steps on the station screen. The warehouse handles material in and out in the same system.

  3. You track and report

    Where each work order is, how much material is left, which part had a problem: all on one screen.

Who it's for

Big ERP systems are heavy and expensive, and Excel is not enough. QY Systems is for the manufacturers in between.

  • Makers of serial-numbered or lot-tracked parts
  • Suppliers whose customers ask for quality records and certificates of conformity
  • Composites, machinery, industrial equipment and similar job-shop production
  • Teams that keep production records on paper forms or in Excel today

Your data stays in your factory

The system can run on a computer in your own factory. Your production data is not sent anywhere else; tablets and computers on the floor connect through a web browser. We set up backups and updates.
