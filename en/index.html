Products

Two modules, one record.

Production and Inventory work on their own; together, material used in production is deducted from stock automatically.

Production (MES)

Shows where every part is, from work order to shipment.

  • Work orders, operation routing and station tracking
  • A serial number per part; scrap and rework records
  • Work orders by customer and due date
  • Large screens on the floor, detailed tables in the office

Inventory

Tells you which rack, which lot and how much.

  • Balances by material, lot and rack
  • An unalterable record of every receipt, issue and transfer
  • Stock counts that catch movements made during the count
  • Purchasing and material reservation for customers
